# Workflow Orchestration

**Invocation rule:** Apply this skill only when the student explicitly invokes it or asks for a structured execution workflow, verification loop, decision log, or lessons-learned process. Do not auto-apply it to every answer.

## Purpose

Bring discipline to a multi-step management task. The skill creates a visible plan, a verification step, and a lessons log so the work can be reviewed and improved.

## Inputs

Gather:

- the objective and scope,
- the constraints and decision rights,
- the available data and instructions,
- the tasks and owners,
- the acceptance criteria,
- the review point.

## Procedure

1. **Plan.** State the objective, scope, deliverables, assumptions, tasks, owners, and sequence. Identify the evidence needed for each step.
2. **Set gates.** Define the checkpoints where the work will be reviewed and approved. Include the criteria for passing each gate.
3. **Execute.** Work through the tasks in sequence. Record decisions, assumptions, and blockers as they occur.
4. **Verify.** Check the output against:
   - the student's instructions,
   - the company evidence,
   - the acceptance criteria,
   - the decision rights,
   - the risk and quality standards.
5. **Correct.** If verification fails, state what is wrong, what will change, and who owns the correction.
6. **Capture lessons.** Record what worked, what failed, what should change next time, and what should be standardised.
7. **Close.** Confirm the outcome, the remaining actions, and the review date.

## Output format

Provide:

- a plan table,
- a verification checklist with results,
- a decision and assumption log,
- a lessons-learned note,
- remaining actions and owners.

## Quality checks

- Verification is not skipped.
- Decisions and assumptions are recorded.
- The workflow does not create unnecessary stages.
- The single AI Manager remains accountable; no multi-agent orchestration is required.
- The student's instructions and company data remain authoritative.
- Lessons are specific and actionable.
